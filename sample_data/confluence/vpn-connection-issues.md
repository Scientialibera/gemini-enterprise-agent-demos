# VPN: Cannot connect (SAMPLE ARTICLE)

Applies to: company laptops (Windows and macOS). This is sample content for testing.

## Steps to resolve

1. **Check your internet connection.** Open any public website. If it does not load, fix
   the network first (Wi-Fi off and on, or restart the router).
2. **Restart the VPN client.** Quit the VPN app from the system tray or menu bar, then open
   it again and select the "Corporate" profile.
3. **Sign in again.** If you see "authentication failed", sign out of the VPN client and
   sign in with your company email. Approve the multi-factor prompt within 60 seconds.
4. **Update the client.** Help > Check for updates. Versions older than 6.0 are blocked.
5. **Restart the laptop** if none of the above works.

## Additional notes

- Hotel and airport networks sometimes block VPN ports. Try a phone hotspot.
- If the client shows "certificate expired", do not continue: contact the Service Desk.
